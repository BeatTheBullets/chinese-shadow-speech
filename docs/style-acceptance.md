# Style acceptance (manual, Lacquer restyle)

1. At night, no page or dialog has a light surface, including select menus and sliders.
2. The control bar:
   - ‹ n/N › are together.
   - ▶ Listen plays the speaker.
   - The speaker icon opens Sound, and Space and Enter still work while it is open.
   - The Sound pop-up holds the speed slider, the 0.75× preset (S), the speaker volume and, where it applies (Flow along,
     drills), the guide level.
   - › is greyed out on the last sentence in Echo.
3. ⚑ and 字/pīn/EN sit on the sentence card.
4. Echo:
   - "Listening" while the speaker plays.
   - "Your turn" with a red frame, timer and live waveform that is flat when silent.
   - Auto-stop after you finish ("stops by itself when you finish" shows beside the timer).
   - No page jump: the sentence card and the tracks below stay put between idle, Listening, Your turn and back.
5. Flow:
   - After: 3·2·1 at the end of each clip.
   - Along: 3·2·1 before each new line, and "Speak along" while speaking.
   - No page jump between Listening, Get ready and Your turn: the ring appears over the stage, not above it.
   - Space in the Flow setup (or under the summary) plays the sentence and shows "Listening".
6. Drill shows the same stages and keys (Anki hidden, Next in accent while reviewing a sentence).
7. Only Echo and Flow are offered. Old Chorus and Guided takes still show.
8. Characters are in the serif font.
9. Lesson tiles and the sentence card have the window-frame corners; buttons don't.
10. At night, the Listen wipe is plainly visible: the characters turn blue-grey as the speaker reaches them (Echo and Flow).
11. ⌂ Library offers the end-of-session Anki export (it replaced Finish session); the browser's Back button skips the offer.
12. The help overlay (?) lists the keys as small key caps: ← →, Space, Enter, R, A, S, F, T P E, L and ?; Esc or ? closes it.
13. No key faces as buttons: the tracks' Listen / Me, the Flow setup's Replay run and the lesson list's close are labelled
    buttons with the key as a small grey hint.
